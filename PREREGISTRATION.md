# ORBITA-30 Preregistration (Draft)

Status: not registered with an external repository. This document records the intended order of decisions so later analysis cannot silently change hypotheses after seeing data.

## Confirmatory questions

1. Which item response and forced-choice model best represents the frozen mixed-format data under pre-specified fit, identifiability and predictive criteria?
2. Do the 30 trait scales show acceptable model-based reliability for exploratory developmental reporting, with uncertainty reported rather than hidden?
3. Does the planned factor structure replicate in a held-out sample?
4. Are convergent/discriminant associations and subgroup/language invariance consistent with the approved construct map?
5. Does calibrated adaptive selection reduce uncertainty relative to the fixed core without increasing missingness, burden or subgroup disparity?

## Exclusions and data handling

- Exclude only pre-specified technical invalids, duplicate participation and explicit consent failures; record counts and reasons before outcome analysis.
- Do not use synthetic E2E rows as pilot evidence.
- No population percentile, diagnosis, intelligence, employability or deterministic career claim is an outcome of this registration.
- Current v2 analyses are performed on frozen versions `orbita30_ru_v2`, `core60-v1`, `mvp-0.5-core60`, `mercury-generated-0.1` and `unvalidated-v2`; any model/version change is exploratory until re-registered. Historical v1 analyses retain their original version tuple and are not pooled with v2.

## Planned reporting

Report sample flow, missingness, quality flags, item/pair behavior, reliability, factor structure, validity, fairness/DIF, invariance, calibration, adaptive information and limitations. Report null/negative results and confidence/credible intervals. Keep norming as a separate protocol and do not pool languages before invariance evidence.
